    About Blacko

    Built for the site,
    not the shelf.

    Blacko makes holster work trousers and shorts for every trade. Every tool where your hand expects it, so the day goes on the job, not on hunting through the van.

    How it started

    From UK sites to Australian ones

    On UK sites, holster trousers are standard kit: pockets that hang off the hip for pencils, knives and drivers, knee-pad pockets for the low jobs and zipped pockets that keep your phone safe on a ladder.

    On Australian sites, the same trousers kept getting noticed. So we started bringing them over, made for Australian trades.

    “People kept commenting about the trousers I was wearing.”

    Founder, Blacko Workwear

    What we stand for

    • 01

      Holster first

      The trousers are designed around the pockets, not the other way round. Tools on hand, hands free.

    • 02

      Every trade

      Sparkies, chippies, tilers, arborists and everyone in between. One cut, eight colours.

    • 03

      UK style, Aussie sites

      The holster layout UK trades swear by, brought to Australia and shipped free anywhere in the country.
